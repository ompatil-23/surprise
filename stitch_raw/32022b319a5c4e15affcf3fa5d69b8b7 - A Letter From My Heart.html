My Dearest Maya,

If I had a thousand lifetimes across a thousand galaxies, I would search for your laughter in every single one. You possess this quiet magic; you make ordinary mornings feel like sacred poetry, and dark nights feel safe and warm.

Watching you grow, dream, and illuminate every space around you is the greatest privilege of my life. You make the world softer, brighter, and infinitely more kind simply by being who you are. Your heart is an endless sanctuary, and your courage inspires me to be a better human being with every sunrise.

Thank you for being my anchor, my sweetest adventure, and my eternal favorite person. Happy Birthday, my love.

Forever & unconditionally yours,
